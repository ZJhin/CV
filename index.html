Profile

Southern Ocean questions, model-scale evidence.

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Tasmania and the Australian Antarctic Program Partnership, and a member of COSIMA. My work investigates how Antarctic sea-ice processes shape water-mass transformation, freshwater redistribution, ocean ventilation, and carbon uptake across CMIP6 climate models.

Research

From sea-ice physics to the ocean carbon sink.

My research connects process diagnostics with climate-model evaluation, using density-binned analysis and inter-model comparison to understand why models diverge.

Sea-Ice-Driven WMT

Quantifying transformation pathways linked to Antarctic sea-ice growth, melt, brine rejection, and freshwater redistribution.

Ventilation & Freshwater

Diagnosing how surface forcing and stratification regulate Southern Ocean ventilation and exchange with interior waters.

Carbon Uptake

Testing how Southern Ocean physical processes modulate air-sea CO2 fluxes and carbon uptake across CMIP6 models.
